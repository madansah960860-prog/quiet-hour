About it

A lap tray only works if it is flat and wide enough. This one is 21 by 13 inches, which takes a 1000-piece puzzle box lid, a laptop up to 15 inches, or a mug and a plate with room left over. The surface is level rather than dished, because a dished tray is useless for anything with a base.

The legs fold under and click into a detent, so the tray sits flat for storage and at 9 inches high when open. There is a 0.6-inch raised lip on all four sides that stops a rolling pencil or a slipping mug going over the edge, and two cut-out handles at the ends.

Bamboo is a grass rather than a hardwood, which is why the tray is light — 2.8 pounds — without being flimsy. It is finished with a food-safe mineral oil, not a lacquer, so it can be re-oiled rather than refinished when it dries out.

Care
  • Wipe with a damp cloth and dry at once. Never put it in a dishwasher and never soak it.
  • Re-oil with food-safe mineral oil when the surface starts to look pale or dry, roughly twice a year.
  • Do not put a hot pan straight onto it; use a trivet.
  • Store flat with the legs folded.
