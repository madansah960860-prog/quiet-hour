Standard shipping is $7.50 per order anywhere in the United States, and it is free once your subtotal passes $59. There is no handling charge and nothing is added at the final step. Sales tax is calculated at checkout where applicable. The shipping policy has the full detail.

We pack most orders within one to two business days, and the marble bookends and the cast iron teapot within two to three. Ground service then usually takes three to six business days, and expedited one to three. Those transit windows are the carrier's estimate rather than a promise. If we cannot ship by the date we gave you, we will write to you on or before that date with a revised date and your right to cancel for a full refund.
