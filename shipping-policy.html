Destinations we post to

We post within the United States and nowhere else. That covers all fifty states, the District of Columbia and Puerto Rico. Alaska, Hawaii and Puerto Rico pay the same flat rate as everybody else and reach the free-shipping threshold on the same terms; the only difference is that ground transit takes noticeably longer, and your confirmation email will say so.

PO boxes and APO, FPO and DPO military addresses are fine and go by United States Postal Service. There are two exceptions. The Marble Bookends (9.2 lb the pair) and the Cast Iron Teapot (2.9 lb, in a bulky protective carton) are outside the size and weight limits for those services and need a street address. If one of them is ordered to a PO box we will telephone you rather than cancelling quietly behind your back.

We do not post to addresses outside the United States, we do not post to freight forwarders, and a United States billing card does not change either of those.

Cost

Standard shipping is $7.50 per order, whatever is in it and wherever in the country it is going. Once your subtotal reaches $59 before tax, standard shipping is free. Expedited shipping is a flat $17.50 per order and has no free threshold.

Nothing else is added: no handling charge, no packaging charge, no fuel surcharge, no minimum order and no charge for a gift note. What the cart shows you is what you pay, plus sales tax where it applies.

One charge per order
An order that needs two boxes still pays one shipping charge, and you may receive two tracking numbers.
Heavy items
The bookends and the cast iron teapot cost us considerably more than $7.50 to send. We carry that difference rather than springing an oversize fee on you at the last step.

The two clocks

It helps to keep these apart, because people often quote us one when they mean the other.

How long we take to pack

- Books, bookmarks and tea: 1 business day - Puzzles, the lap tray, the lamp, the mug, the glass teapot: 1 to 2 business days - Marble Bookends and Cast Iron Teapot: 2 to 3 business days - Anything ordered after 1:00pm Central Time, at a weekend, or on a federal holiday starts its clock on the next business day

How long the carrier takes

These are the carriers' own estimates for time in transit once the parcel has left us. They are estimates, not guarantees, and they exclude our packing time above.

  • Ground, lower forty-eight: 3 to 6 business days
  • Ground, Alaska, Hawaii and Puerto Rico: 6 to 12 business days
  • Expedited, lower forty-eight only: 1 to 3 business days
  • USPS to PO boxes and APO/FPO/DPO: 4 to 10 business days, and a military address can take a good deal longer once it enters the military postal system, which no carrier can track

Put together, a standard order to a street address in the lower forty-eight normally arrives four to eight business days after you place it.

Who carries it, and tracking

We use USPS Ground Advantage, USPS Priority Mail, UPS Ground and UPS 2nd Day Air, choosing on size, weight and destination. We do not offer a carrier choice at checkout because for most of our parcels it would change neither the price nor the day it lands.

You will get two emails. The first confirms the order. The second, when the parcel leaves us, gives the carrier and the tracking number. Tracking normally starts moving within a day of that email — a number that has not updated overnight is ordinary and not a sign of trouble.

If four business days pass with no shipping confirmation, something has gone wrong here. Telephone (608) 555-0167 or email care@quiethourshop.com and we will find out what and tell you honestly.

Late orders and your right to cancel

The Federal Trade Commission's Mail, Internet, or Telephone Order Merchandise Rule sets out what a seller must do when it cannot ship on time. Rather than leave that implied, here is exactly what we will do.

  • We only state shipping times we have a reasonable basis to meet. Where no time is stated at all, we will ship within 30 days of receiving a properly completed order.
  • If we are going to miss the date we gave you, we will contact you on or before that date. The notice will give either a definite new shipping date or a plain statement that we cannot give one, and it will tell you that you may cancel for a full refund at no cost to you.
  • Where the new date is 30 days or less after the original, you need not reply. Staying silent counts as agreeing to the short delay, and you can still cancel at any point before it ships.
  • Where the new date is more than 30 days after the original, or where we cannot give a date, the order is cancelled and refunded automatically unless you tell us you would rather wait.
  • If we then miss the revised date too, we will write again beforehand — and this time silence is not agreement. Without your express say-so the order is cancelled and refunded.
  • Cancelling takes one line. Reply to the notice, telephone (608) 555-0167, or email care@quiethourshop.com. We will not ask you to justify it.

A cancellation for delay is refunded to the original payment method within 7 working days, in full, with nothing deducted.

When something goes wrong in transit

### Damaged on arrival Email care@quiethourshop.com within 14 days of delivery with the order number and a photograph of the item and of the outer carton. We send a replacement where we have stock and refund in full where we do not, and the postage is ours in both cases. Please hold onto the packaging until we have replied; the carrier sometimes asks for it. You need not send the damaged item back unless we ask.

Missing pieces

A puzzle short of a piece is a faulty puzzle. Tell us within the return window and we send a complete replacement. We will not ask you to count the remainder and we will not make you wait while we chase the manufacturer for one piece.

Marked delivered, nothing there

Check with anyone else at the address and the usual alternative spots first; most turn up within a day. If it has not appeared after three business days, tell us. We open the carrier claim ourselves and, whatever the carrier concludes, you get a replacement or a refund. Chasing a carrier is our job, not yours.

Tracking has stalled

If nothing has moved for seven consecutive business days, let us know and we will open a lost-in-transit claim. Replacement or refund, whichever you prefer.

We sent the wrong thing

Our error. A prepaid label and the right item go out together, and you are not out of pocket for anything.

Getting the address right

Please check the delivery address before you order. If a parcel comes back to us as undeliverable because the address was wrong or incomplete, we refund the goods in full and deduct only the actual return-transit cost the carrier charged us, shown to you on the carrier's invoice. Where the mistake is ours, we send it again at our own cost.

We cannot change an address once a parcel has shipped. Spot it beforehand and telephone us — we can fix it up to the moment it leaves.

Sales tax

Sales tax is worked out at checkout from your delivery address, where applicable, and appears as its own line before you confirm. It is never folded into the product price or the shipping charge. As a Wisconsin business we collect Wisconsin sales and use tax, and we collect in other states where economic-nexus rules require it.
