About it

Light for reading wants to be warm and it wants to come from the side, not from overhead. This lamp does both: a 14-inch gooseneck that holds whatever position you bend it to, and a 2700K warm-white LED rather than the blue-white that most clip lamps ship with.

There are three brightness steps on a touch pad at the base — roughly 120, 300 and 550 lumens — and the lamp remembers the last one you used. The head is 3 inches across with a frosted diffuser, so the pool of light has a soft edge instead of a hard ring.

It comes with both a weighted round base and a screw clamp that fits a surface up to 2.2 inches thick, so it can stand on a side table or grip the edge of one. It runs off a 5-foot USB-C cable and the plug adapter is in the box. The LED is not user-replaceable, which is worth knowing: rated life is 25,000 hours, and at three hours a night that is about twenty years, but when it goes the lamp is done.
