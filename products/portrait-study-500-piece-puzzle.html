About it

This is the one to start somebody on. Five hundred pieces sounds modest, but these are large-format pieces — roughly 1.2 inches across against the 0.85 inches of a standard 500 — so the finished puzzle is the same 27 by 20 inches as a 1000, and every piece is easy to pick up and easy to see.

The image is a painted portrait with strong brush texture, which helps: brushwork gives you direction and edge to follow even inside a single colour, in a way a photograph does not.

Large-format pieces are often sold with language about age or ability attached to them. We are not going to do that. They are simply bigger pieces, and plenty of people prefer them for no reason other than that they would rather not squint.
