Four plain facts

No star ratings and no testimonials anywhere on this site. We have not been trading long enough to have honest ones, so we show none.

  • Type sizes in points, not adjectives

    Every book on this site lists its actual point size and grid measurements. "Large print" means nothing on its own, so we give you the numbers and let you decide.

  • Weights stated before you order

    The cast iron teapot is 4.4 pounds full and the marble bookends are 9.2 pounds the pair. Those numbers are on the product pages because for some people they are the deciding factor.

  • 45 days to send it back

    Unopened and unused, 45 days from delivery, change of mind included. If the fault is ours the return postage is ours too, and there is never a restocking fee.

  • One shipping price, written down

    $7.50 anywhere in the United States, free over $59. No handling charge and nothing added at the final step. Sales tax is calculated at checkout where applicable.

Where this came from

The Quiet Hour started because one of us kept being asked the same question by relatives at Christmas: what do you buy for somebody who reads, and already owns plenty of books? The answers turned out to be small and specific — a lamp that puts light on the page instead of in your eyes, a puzzle with pieces you can see, a mug that holds a proper cup.

So that became the shop. Fifteen things, described in full, with the measurements that usually get left out. We are not trying to be a department store and we are not adding a product a week. If something goes on this list it is because we could not find a good reason to leave it off.
