About it

Buying puzzle magazines one at a time in a shop is how most people discover which kind they actually like. This bundle skips that: six different titles, all large-print, so you can find out in one go whether you are a word-search person or a logic-grid person.

The six are a word search, a general knowledge crossword, a cryptic crossword, a number-logic book, a word-fit book, and a mixed puzzler. Between them there are roughly 320 puzzles. All six are set at 18 point or larger, and all six have their solutions in the back.

These are current-issue newsstand magazines, so the specific issue numbers change through the year and we cannot promise a particular one. What we can promise is six different titles, all large-print, never a duplicate within a bundle.
