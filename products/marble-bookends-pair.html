About it

Bookends fail for one reason: they are not heavy enough. A hollow metal one will skate across a shelf the moment you pull a book out of the middle. These are solid marble, 4.6 pounds each, and they do not move.

Each is cut as an L — a 5 by 5 inch upright on a 4 inch foot — so the weight of the books themselves presses the foot down. The whole base is felted, which protects a shelf and adds the last bit of grip.

Marble is a natural stone. Veining, colour depth and the exact pattern differ from block to block, and the pair you receive will not be identical twins. That is how the material is, and we would rather say so than photograph a matched pair and disappoint you.

Care
  • Dust with a dry or barely damp cloth.
  • Do not use vinegar, lemon, or any acidic or abrasive cleaner — acid etches marble permanently.
  • Lift rather than drag, even with the felt base.
  • Marble chips if dropped. It is stone, not plastic.
