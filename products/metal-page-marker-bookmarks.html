About it

A bookmark that falls out is not a bookmark. These are sprung steel clips with a brass electroplate: the tail slides over the page and the spring holds it, so the book can go in a bag upside down and the marker stays where you put it.

Each one is 2.6 inches long and 0.7 inches wide, with a different pierced pattern at the head — an oak leaf, a compass rose, a wave, and a plain bar for people who would rather not have a pattern. The edges are rolled rather than stamped, which is what stops a metal bookmark scoring the page opposite.

They are 0.4mm thick, so four of them in one book will not splay the spine. They come on a printed card, which makes the set an easy thing to put in an envelope.

Care
  • Wipe with a dry cloth. Do not use metal polish — it takes the lacquer off the plate.
  • Keep them dry; the plate will spot if they are left damp.
  • Do not bend the spring open past its rest position or it will not grip.
