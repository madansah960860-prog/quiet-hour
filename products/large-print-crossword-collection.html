About it

The type size is the point of this book, so here are the actual numbers rather than the word "large": the clues are set at 22 point, the grid squares are 0.55 inches across, and the clue numbers inside the squares are 11 point. A standard newspaper crossword runs about 8 point with 0.3-inch squares.

There are eighty puzzles, all American-style — fully interlocking, every letter crossing — graded across four difficulty bands of twenty puzzles each, with the easiest first. Solutions are in the back, upside down, one page per four puzzles.

It is printed on 100gsm uncoated paper rather than the usual 70gsm newsprint, which matters for two reasons: a pen does not bleed through to the puzzle behind it, and the page does not grey out the white squares. The binding is a lay-flat perfect bind, so the book stays open on a table without being held.
