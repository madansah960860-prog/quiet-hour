About it

The reason to own a glass teapot is that you can see when the tea is ready, and the reason to own one with a lift-out basket is that you can stop it being ready any further. Leaf left sitting in the pot keeps brewing and turns bitter; a basket you can actually remove solves it.

The body is borosilicate glass, which is the heat-resistant kind — it takes boiling water poured straight in without a warm-up. Capacity is 27 fluid ounces, which is two large mugs or three teacups. The basket is 18/8 stainless with a fine mesh, 2.4 inches across and 3.5 inches deep, so leaf has room to open rather than being packed into a ball.

The lid has a silicone-rimmed knob and the spout has a drip cut. Borosilicate is heat resistant, not shatterproof: it will break if you drop it, and it should not go on a direct flame or a ceramic hob.

Care
  • Hand wash in warm soapy water. The glass is dishwasher safe on the top rack, but the silicone seal lasts longer washed by hand.
  • Microwave safe without the basket in it. Never put the stainless basket in a microwave.
  • Not for stovetop, hob or open flame.
  • Descale with a warm white-vinegar solution if hard water leaves a film, then rinse well.
