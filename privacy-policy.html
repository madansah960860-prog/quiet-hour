Scope

This notice describes how Quiet Hour Trading Co. handles personal information collected through quiethourshop.com, through our order emails, through the mailing list, and over the telephone on (608) 555-0167.

We are a fifteen-product shop run by two people. We are not in the advertising business: this site carries no analytics tag, no advertising pixel, no social embed and no third-party script of any kind. Those are facts about how the pages are built, verifiable by viewing the source, not promises about intent.

What we collect

### Things you type or tell us Order details :: Name, delivery address, billing address, email address, telephone number. Needed to make up a parcel, address it, and tell you it has gone. Payment details :: Entered on the payment processor's own hosted page. Card numbers never reach our servers and are never stored by us. We see the last four digits, the card brand, whether the authorisation succeeded, and the amount — which is what we need to match a payment to an order and to refund it. Messages :: Whatever you put in the contact form or in an email: your name, your email address, an order number if you give one, and the message itself. Mailing list :: Your email address and the date you asked to join. Telephone orders :: The same order details, written down by hand and then typed into the order system.

Things collected without you typing them

Our host keeps ordinary server logs — requesting IP address, timestamp, the file asked for, the HTTP status, the referring page and the browser user-agent string. They exist for security and for diagnosing faults. They are kept 30 days and then deleted.

The site sets no cookies at all, first-party or third-party. Your cart is held in your browser's localStorage, which is a storage feature of the browser and not a cookie: it stays on your device, it is never transmitted to us, and clearing your browser data erases it. We cannot see what is in anybody's cart.

What we use it for

- Making up, addressing and sending your parcel, and dealing with any return or refund. - Sending transactional email: order confirmation, shipping confirmation with tracking, a delay notice if one becomes necessary, and refund confirmations. - Replying to your question, or telephoning you back. - Sending the occasional letter, if you asked for it and only until you ask us to stop. - Keeping the records that tax and accounting law require. - Spotting and preventing fraudulent orders.

We do not build advertising profiles, we do not do automated decision-making, and we do not profile customers in any sense.

Cookies, and why there is no banner

There is no cookie banner on this site because there are no cookies to consent to. Nothing is tracking you here.

Two browser features are used and both stay on your own machine: localStorage, holding your cart, and your browser's normal HTTP cache, holding our stylesheet and images so that a second page loads quickly.

We honour Global Privacy Control signals and Do Not Track headers by default, in the straightforward sense that there is no cross-context tracking here for them to switch off.

Who else sees your information

Only the handful of service providers it takes to actually send a parcel. Each is engaged as a service provider or processor and may use the information only to do that job, never for its own purposes.

Payment processor
Takes your name, billing address, email and card details directly from you on its own hosted page, to take payment and issue refunds.
Carriers, USPS and UPS
Take your name, delivery address, telephone number and parcel weight, to deliver it and provide tracking.
Email provider
Takes your email address and the content of transactional emails and of the mailing letter.
Web host
Holds the site files and the 30-day server logs described above.
Accountant and tax authorities
Take order and payment records, as the law requires.

We will also disclose information when we are legally obliged to — a valid subpoena, court order or lawful demand — and where we are permitted to tell you, we will. If the business were ever sold, customer records could pass to the buyer as part of it; we would email everybody affected before that happened.

We have never sold personal information and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising. Not for anyone, and not for anyone under 16.

Your rights, wherever you live

Several states now give residents privacy rights. Rather than checking your address first, we honour all of the following for every customer.

  • To know — what categories and specific pieces of information we hold, where they came from, why we have them, and who we have passed them to.
  • To delete — subject only to records that tax, accounting and fraud-prevention law require us to keep.
  • To correct — anything inaccurate.
  • To opt out of sale or sharing — there is nothing to opt out of, because we do neither. That is why you will not find a "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" link on this site: the practice it would disable does not happen here.
  • To limit use of sensitive personal information — we do not collect any as the statutes define it: no Social Security number, no financial account number, no precise geolocation, no biometric, genetic or health data.
  • Not to be discriminated against — exercising any of these will never affect your price, your service or whether we accept your order.
  • To appeal — if we refuse a request we will explain why and how to appeal, and one of the owners will review it personally.

These apply to residents of California under the CCPA as amended by the CPRA, and on substantially similar terms to residents of Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Utah, Texas, Oregon, Montana and other states with comprehensive privacy legislation.

Making a request

Email care@quiethourshop.com with Privacy request in the subject line, or telephone (608) 555-0167 during Monday to Friday, 8:30am to 4:30pm Central Time. Say which right you are exercising and give the email address or order number you used so that we can find you.

We acknowledge within 10 days and answer substantively within 45 days. Where we genuinely need longer we will tell you inside those 45 days and take up to a further 45, never more than 90 days in total. An authorised agent acting for you will be asked for written authority and we will verify directly with you.

Requests are free. If one were manifestly unfounded or excessive we could charge a reasonable fee, but we would explain and agree it with you first rather than simply invoicing.

How long things are kept

Order records :: 7 years, as tax and accounting rules require. Payment records (last four digits only) :: 7 years, alongside the order. Contact form messages and email :: 24 months from the last message, then deleted. Mailing list address :: Until you unsubscribe, then removed within 10 days. Server logs :: 30 days. Return and refund records :: 7 years, alongside the order.

Keeping it safe

The site is served over HTTPS so traffic between your browser and us is encrypted. Card details are entered on the processor's own PCI-DSS compliant page and never touch our systems. Order records are reachable only by the two owners, each with a separate account and multi-factor authentication. The few paper records we keep are locked.

No system is perfectly secure, and we are not going to claim otherwise. Were a breach to affect your personal information, we would notify you and the relevant state authorities without undue delay, as state breach-notification law requires.

Children

This is a general-audience shop selling puzzles, books and tea. It is not directed to children under 13, we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13, and nothing here is designed to appeal particularly to children.

If we discover we hold information from a child under 13 we will delete it promptly. If you believe a child has given us information, email care@quiethourshop.com and we will remove it and write back to confirm that we have.

Ordering requires you to be 18 or over — see the terms of service.

If this notice changes

The in-force date at the top always reflects the current version. For any change that materially affects your rights we will email the mailing list and put a notice on the home page for 30 days, and we will not apply a materially worse policy retroactively to information already collected.
