About The Quiet Hour

Two people, fifteen products and a converted shop unit in Madison, Wisconsin.

Who we are

The Quiet Hour is a two-person shop run out of a converted ground-floor unit on Larkspur Avenue in Madison, Wisconsin. We are the only two people who touch an order: one of us picks it and packs it, the other answers the telephone, and on a busy Monday we swap.

We are a retailer, not a manufacturer. Everything here is bought in from publishers, importers and small makers, and we say so plainly rather than implying a workshop out the back that does not exist.

How we choose what to put on the shelf

Fifteen products is not an accident. It is what two people can honestly know well. A thing earns a place here by getting past three questions.

  1. Can we give you the number instead of the adjective? "Large print" is not information. Twenty-two point clues in a 0.55-inch grid is information. If a supplier cannot tell us the measurement, we do not stock the product.
  2. Is the trade-off stated? Cast iron holds heat for forty minutes and weighs four and a half pounds full. Wooden puzzle pieces feel wonderful and cost more than board. Both halves of that go on the page, not just the flattering half.
  3. Would we keep using it? Everything on this list has been in one of our two houses for at least a season. That is not a guarantee it will suit you, but it does mean nobody is describing a product they have only seen a photograph of.

Things that do not get on the shelf: anything sold mainly on packaging, anything whose only virtue is being cheap, and anything we would have to be vague about to make sound good.

Where orders ship from

Every parcel is packed and posted from 880 Larkspur Avenue, Madison, Wisconsin. We ship within the United States only — all fifty states, the District of Columbia and Puerto Rico — and we ship to PO boxes and to APO, FPO and DPO addresses. Details, costs and timings are on the shipping policy page.

Most orders leave within one to two business days. Two do not: the marble bookends and the cast iron teapot are heavy and take two to three business days, because they are packed differently and go by ground.

What we will not tell you

Being clear about this is part of the job.

  • No health claims about the tea. Tea is a drink. We will tell you the leaf grade, the origin region the importer gave us, the water temperature and the steeping time. We will not tell you it calms you down, aids your digestion, or does anything at all to your body, because we cannot substantiate that and it would be an unlawful claim.
  • No claims about puzzles and the mind. Puzzles are enjoyable. We do not say they keep your brain sharp, delay anything, improve memory, or have any effect on cognition. Those are health claims dressed as marketing and they have no place on a puzzle box.
  • No reviews or star ratings. There are none on this site. We have no honest review data yet, and manufacturing some would breach the Federal Trade Commission's rules on consumer reviews as well as being contemptible.
  • No condescension about large print. Large-print books are set in large print. They are not "for" anyone in particular. Plenty of people simply prefer not to squint after supper, and we are not going to imply anything else about why you might want one.
  • No country-of-origin claims. We do not print "Made in USA" on anything, because we do not hold the documentation the FTC standard requires. We can tell you where a parcel ships from, which is a different statement.
  • No urgency tricks. No countdown clocks, no stock counters, no crossed-out prices we never charged.

Accessibility commitment

This site is built to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines version 2.1 at Level AA, and it is tested rather than assumed.

Body text is set at 18 pixels with a line height of 1.75 and there is no light-grey small print anywhere on the site. Text contrast is at least 4.5 to 1 and in most cases better than 7 to 1. Every control is a real button or a real link, operable from the keyboard, with a visible focus outline. A skip-to-content link is the first thing in the tab order on every page. Every image has meaningful alternative text, and every image has width and height attributes so nothing jumps around as the page loads. The layout reflows to a 320-pixel screen without horizontal scrolling and remains usable at 200 per cent zoom.

There is nothing that moves on its own: no carousels, no pop-ups on load, no infinite scroll, no animation you cannot escape.

If anything here is awkward for you to use, telephone (608) 555-0167 or email care@quiethourshop.com. We will take the order over the telephone and we will fix the page.
