About it

A whimsy puzzle is one where a good proportion of the pieces are cut as identifiable objects rather than as interlocking tabs, and it changes the whole experience: you are half solving a puzzle and half finding things in it. Sixty-eight of these two hundred pieces are whimsies.

Everything is laser-cut from 3mm birch plywood with no two pieces alike and no grid at all, which is possible in wood and not in board. Because the edge pieces are also irregular, you cannot start with the border in the usual way — most people start from the whimsies and work outward.

Two hundred pieces takes most people an evening. At 12 by 9 inches finished it fits on a tray or a side table, which is the other reason to own one.
