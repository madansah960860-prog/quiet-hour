In one paragraph

You have 45 days from delivery to send something back, and changing your mind is a perfectly good reason. It needs to be unused, unopened where it was sealed, and in its original packaging. Where the fault is ours we pay the postage both ways and refund everything including your original shipping. Where you simply changed your mind, you pay the return postage and we refund the goods. There is no restocking fee, ever.

The rest of this page is the detail. If anything is unclear, telephone (608) 555-0167 and ask before you send anything.

Why forty-five days

Most shops give thirty. We give 45 because of what we sell: puzzles, books and tea are bought as presents, often weeks ahead of the birthday or the holiday they are meant for, and a thirty-day window can run out before the box is even opened. Forty-five days from delivery covers almost every case of that kind.

The clock runs from the carrier's delivery date, not from the order date and not from the day you opened the parcel. Your request has to reach us inside the 45 days; the parcel itself then has a further 14 days to reach us.

What condition it has to come back in

- Unused. Not assembled, not written in, not brewed, not clamped to a table. - Unopened where it was sealed. Puzzle inner bags, tea tins and shrink-wrapped books must still be sealed. - Complete. Every piece, both bases, the poster, the brewing card, the box it arrived in. - Undamaged by you. Handling it while you decided is expected. A cracked tin or a creased cover is not.

The outer shipping carton does not need to be pristine — you were always going to open it. The product's own box does, because that is what protects it on the way back and what the next customer would receive.

If something comes back used or incomplete we photograph it, email you, and offer either a partial refund reflecting the condition or the item returned to you at our cost. We do not keep it and refuse the refund.

What we cannot take back

- Puzzles with a broken inner seal. A puzzle whose bag has been opened cannot honestly be sold to anybody else as new, and we will not quietly resell one. Sealed puzzles are fully returnable. - Opened tea tins. Loose leaf is a foodstuff. Once a tin's inner press lid has been lifted we cannot take it back. Unopened tins in the sampler are returnable as a complete set. - Books that have been written in. A puzzle book with three crosswords completed is not resaleable. Books still in the state they arrived in are fully returnable. - Items marked final sale. There are none on this site at present. If there ever are, the product page will say so in plain words directly above the Add to Cart button.

None of this applies to anything faulty. A puzzle missing a piece, a cracked mug, a lamp that does not light, a split tea tin — all returnable whatever state they are in and however the seal looks.

Starting a return

1. Email care@quiethourshop.com with Return in the subject line, inside 45 days of delivery. Or telephone (608) 555-0167 during Monday to Friday, 8:30am to 4:30pm Central Time and we will do it with you on the call. 2. Give us your order number (of the form QH-20418, in your confirmation email), which item, how many, and whether it is faulty or a change of mind. A photograph if it is faulty. 3. We reply within one business day with a Returns Number, the address to send to, and — where the fault is ours — a prepaid label attached as a PDF. 4. Write the Returns Number on the outside of the parcel. Pack the item in its own box inside an outer carton and send it with a tracked service; we cannot refund a parcel that never arrives. 5. Send us the tracking number and we will confirm when it reaches us.

Please do not post anything back without a Returns Number. It is not red tape: unlabelled parcels arrive at a unit that also takes in stock deliveries, and they get mislaid.

Postage: who pays

We pay, and we refund your original shipping too :: It arrived damaged. It is faulty or fails in normal use inside the window. A puzzle is missing pieces. We sent the wrong item. The item is materially different from its description here. You pay, and we refund the price of the goods :: You changed your mind. It was not the right present after all. It is heavier than you expected and the weight was stated on the page. You ordered two and needed one.

A change-of-mind return typically costs $7 to $12 for books, puzzles and small items, and $18 to $30 for the bookends or the cast iron teapot, paid by you straight to the carrier. We take no cut of it and we do not mark it up.

If your order originally qualified for free shipping over $59 and returning part of it drops the kept portion below $59, we will not go back and charge you the $7.50. Clawing that back after the fact is a mean trick and we do not play it.

How and when you get the money

Refunds go back to the original payment method, because that is what preserves your card's chargeback protection. We cannot refund to a different card or send a cheque.

Cancelled before it shipped
In full, including shipping, within 7 working days of the cancellation.
Returned, our fault
In full, including your original shipping, within 5 business days of the parcel arriving with us.
Returned, change of mind
Price of the goods, within 5 business days of arrival. Original shipping is not refunded.
Damaged on arrival, refund rather than replacement
In full including shipping, within 3 business days of us seeing your photographs. You do not wait for the item to travel back.

Once we have released a refund your bank decides how quickly it appears — usually three to five business days, occasionally a whole statement cycle. That part is outside our hands, but we will give you the refund reference to pass on if your bank asks.

Exchanges

We do not run a formal exchange. With fifteen products it is slower than the obvious alternative: place a new order for what you want, and return the first under this policy. That way the replacement is already travelling while the original comes back, instead of the two happening one after the other.

Where the exchange is needed because we sent the wrong thing, none of that applies — the correct item goes out immediately, you place nothing and you pay nothing.

Faulty and damaged goods

Report transit damage within 14 days of delivery with photographs of the item and the outer carton. For something that fails in ordinary use, tell us as soon as it happens and within the 45-day window. We will ask what happened and look at photographs, and where it is a manufacturing fault we replace or refund at our cost.

Some things are not faults, and it is fairer to name them now: marble chipped by being dropped, a bamboo tray warped after being soaked or put in a dishwasher, a glass teapot cracked on a hob, cast iron enamel crazed by being put over a flame, and plywood puzzle pieces swollen after getting wet. Each of those is spelled out as a care instruction on the product page, and following those instructions is what keeps the item covered.

Cancelling an order

Before it ships, telephone (608) 555-0167 or email care@quiethourshop.com with the order number. We cancel it and refund in full within 7 working days, and we will not ask why.

Once there is a tracking number the parcel has gone and it becomes a return. You can refuse delivery if you prefer — it comes back to us and we refund the goods — but refused parcels take two or three weeks to find their way home, so an email is quicker.

If you are not satisfied with the outcome

Email care@quiethourshop.com with Escalation in the subject line and your order number. One of the two of us will read it personally and reply within two business days. There is no department to be passed to; there are two of us.

Nothing on this page limits any right you have under Wisconsin consumer law or the consumer protection law of your own state.
