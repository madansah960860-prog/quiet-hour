About it

Wooden puzzles behave differently from cardboard ones and it is worth knowing why before you buy your first. The pieces are 3mm birch plywood rather than pressed board, so they have real thickness, they click rather than slump, and they do not soften if a cup gets knocked over. They also cost more, which is the honest trade.

This one is a political world map with the countries as of the printing year, 500 pieces, finishing at 20 by 14 inches. The cut is a laser cut with a genuinely irregular grid, so you cannot fall back on counting rows, and there are eighteen whimsy pieces cut into recognisable shapes — a whale, a sailing boat, a lighthouse — which is a wooden-puzzle tradition rather than a gimmick.

There is no poster in the box; the lid is the reference image. The lid photograph is printed at the full finished size for exactly that reason.

Care
  • Keep it dry. Plywood will swell if it gets wet and the pieces will stop fitting.
  • Wipe the pieces with a barely damp cloth if they get marked, and dry at once.
  • Store the box flat rather than on its end so the pieces do not all migrate to one corner.
