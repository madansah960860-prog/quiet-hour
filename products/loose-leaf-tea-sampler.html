About it

Three tins, chosen so that they are genuinely different from one another rather than three shades of the same thing. A second-flush Assam, which is a strong malty black tea that takes milk. A Chinese Gunpowder green, rolled into pellets that open as they steep. And a caffeine-free rooibos and orange peel infusion for the evening.

Each tin holds 50 grams, which is roughly 20 to 25 cups depending on how you make it, so the set is about 65 cups altogether. The tins are lined steel with a double lid — an inner press lid that seals and an outer slip lid — because loose leaf goes stale from air long before it goes stale from time.

Every tin has its leaf grade, its origin region as given to us by the importer, the water temperature and the steeping time printed on the label, because those three things make more difference than anything else. We make no claim about what tea does for you. It is a drink, and these three are good ones.

Care
  • Keep the tins closed, cool, dry and away from strong smells. Tea takes up odours.
  • Do not refrigerate. Condensation ruins loose leaf.
  • Use within about 12 months of opening for the black and the herbal, 6 months for the green.
  • Wash the tins in warm water only, and dry completely before refilling.
