About it

A mixed book is the right answer when you do not want to do the same thing for an hour. This one rotates through six puzzle types — word search, quick crossword, word ladder, number fill, spot-the-difference in line art, and anagram rounds — so no two consecutive pages ask the same thing of you.

Everything is set at 20 point or larger, with word-search grids at 0.5 inches per cell and crossword grids at 0.55. There are 96 puzzles across 120 pages, with the solutions in the back, and an index at the front if you want to go straight to one kind.

The paper is 90gsm uncoated, heavier than a magazine and lighter than the crossword collection, which keeps the book down to 12 ounces — light enough to hold rather than rest on a table.
